🔬

E9 "Does not exist"

Naming trivia

Japan's Shinkansen does not have official "E9 series" passenger trains.
The prefix "9" is reserved for test vehicles/engineering vehicles (such as E956 ALFA-X).
The new generation of production cars will skip the E9 and be called E10 directly.

🔬 STEM Engineering Lab

Question 1: Why is the front end of the E5 so long?

Air flowing at high speed creates resistance.

Long nose design:
Air → Smooth separation → Noise reduction → Energy saving
(The E5 nose is 15 meters long; the E8 uses a 9-meter arrow-shaped nose; the ALFA-X test is 22 meters)

Question 2: Why do trains need suspension systems?

Reduce vibration and make passengers more comfortable.
The car body tilting device prevents the train from slowing down too much when cornering.

Question 3: Do you want a double-decker train?

E1/E4 MAX uses vertical space to accommodate twice as many people.
However, a double-decker car has a high center of gravity and needs to turn slower - so after E5, it switched to the "long nose + high speed" strategy.
