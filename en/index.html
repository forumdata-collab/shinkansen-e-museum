E9 "Does not exist"

Naming trivia

Japan's Shinkansen does not have official "E9 series" passenger trains.
The prefix "9" is reserved for test vehicles/engineering vehicles (such as E956 ALFA-X).
The new generation of production cars will skip the E9 and be called E10 directly.

🔬 STEM Engineering Lab

Question 1: Why is the front end of the E5 so long?

Air flowing at high speed creates resistance.

Long nose design:
Air → Smooth separation → Noise reduction → Energy saving
(The E5 nose is 15 meters long; the E8 uses a 9-meter arrow-shaped nose; the ALFA-X test is 22 meters)

Question 2: Why do trains need suspension systems?

Reduce vibration and make passengers more comfortable.
The car body tilting device prevents the train from slowing down too much when cornering.

Question 3: Do you want a double-decker train?

E1/E4 MAX uses vertical space to accommodate twice as many people.
However, a double-decker car has a high center of gravity and needs to turn slower - so after E5, it switched to the "long nose + high speed" strategy.

🎓 Professional vocabulary list

Quick understanding of STEM terms

fluid mechanics

The science of how air and liquids flow. The long nose design of the Shinkansen relies on it to reduce resistance and noise.

Active suspension system

Sensors and computers are used to actively control the shock absorbers, automatically tilting the body when cornering, so that passengers are not subject to centrifugal force.

Wheel and Rail Mechanics

Study the mechanics of wheel and rail contact points, affecting stability, wear and safety.

Gauge

The distance between the insides of two rails. The Shinkansen uses 1,435mm standard gauge; the Mini Shinkansen is a narrow gauge local line converted to standard gauge.

ATC (Automatic Train Control)

A safety system that automatically monitors train speed and automatically brakes if it exceeds speed to prevent collisions.

SiC frequency converter

Silicon carbide semiconductor technology is more energy-efficient and has better heat dissipation than traditional silicon. E10 will use it to improve efficiency.
